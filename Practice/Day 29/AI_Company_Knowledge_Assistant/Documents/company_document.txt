Company Employee Documentation

Company Overview:
Our company provides software development, IT services, and technical support to its customers.

Working Hours:
Employees work from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM from Monday to Friday.
The office remains closed on weekends.

Leave Policy:
Employees can apply for leave through the HR portal.
Casual leave should be requested at least one day in advance.
For emergency leave, employees should contact their reporting manager.

Work From Home:
Employees can request work from home with manager approval.
Work from home requests should be submitted through the HR portal.

Attendance:
Employees are required to mark their attendance every working day.
Attendance can be marked through the company's attendance system.

IT Support:
Employees facing technical issues can contact the IT support team.
The IT support team handles problems related to computers, software, network connectivity, and company systems.

HR Support:
Employees can contact the HR department for leave, attendance, salary, and employee policy-related queries.

Salary:
Employee salaries are processed at the end of every month.
For salary-related questions, employees should contact the HR department.

Security Policy:
Employees must keep their company accounts and passwords confidential.
Company credentials should not be shared with other people.

Employee Benefits:
Eligible employees may receive health insurance, paid leave, and other benefits according to company policy.
