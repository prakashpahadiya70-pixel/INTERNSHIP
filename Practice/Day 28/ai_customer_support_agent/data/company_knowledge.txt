COMPANY CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

Company: TechNova Solutions

PRODUCT INFORMATION

TechNova provides AI-powered software solutions for businesses.
Customers can contact support for product-related questions, account issues, and service information.

ACCOUNT SUPPORT

Customers can update their account information from the Account Settings section.
If a customer forgets their password, they can use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page.

ORDER SUPPORT

Customers can check their order status using the Order History section.
Customers should provide their Order ID when contacting support about a specific order.

REFUND POLICY

Customers can request a refund within 7 days of purchase.
Refund requests must include the relevant Order ID.
Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

SUPPORT HOURS

Customer support is available Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.
Support is closed on weekends and public holidays.

FALLBACK POLICY

If the knowledge base does not contain reliable information for a customer question, the AI should not invent an answer.
The system should provide a fallback response and recommend contacting human customer support.
