COMPANY EMPLOYEE POLICY

LEAVE POLICY

Employees can apply for leave through the HR portal.

Employees should select the appropriate leave type, enter the
required leave dates, provide a valid reason, and submit the
request through the HR portal.

Leave requests are reviewed by the employee's reporting manager
and HR department.

For planned leave, employees should submit their request in
advance whenever possible.

For emergency or unexpected leave, employees should inform their
reporting manager as soon as possible.

Employees should check the HR portal for the current status of
their leave request.

WORKING HOURS

The standard working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM,
Monday to Friday.

Employees are expected to follow the company's attendance
and working-hour policies.

REMOTE WORK POLICY

Employees may work remotely when approved by their reporting
manager and according to company policy.

Remote work requests should be submitted through the appropriate
company process.

EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

Employees may be eligible for company benefits according to
their employment terms and company policies.

Employees can contact the HR department for detailed information
about available benefits.

HR SUPPORT

Employees can contact the HR department for questions related
to leave, attendance, benefits, and other employee policies.
