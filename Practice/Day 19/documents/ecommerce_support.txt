E-COMMERCE CUSTOMER SUPPORT KNOWLEDGE BASE

PRODUCTS:
Our store sells smartphones, laptops, headphones, smartwatches and other electronic accessories.
Product availability depends on current inventory.

ORDERING:
Customers can place orders through the online store.
After placing an order, customers receive an order confirmation through email.
Orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.

DELIVERY:
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 7 business days.
Express delivery usually takes 1 to 3 business days.
Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location and product availability.

RETURNS:
Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving an eligible product.
The product must be unused and returned with its original packaging and accessories.
Some products may not be eligible for return.

REFUNDS:
After a returned product is inspected and approved, the refund is initiated.
Refund processing may take 5 to 7 business days depending on the payment method and bank.

PAYMENTS:
Customers can pay using credit cards, debit cards, UPI and supported online payment methods.
If a payment is deducted but the order is not created, the customer should contact customer support.

DAMAGED PRODUCTS:
If a product arrives damaged, the customer should report the issue to customer support as soon as possible.
Customers may be asked to provide photographs or videos of the damaged product.

CUSTOMER SUPPORT:
Customer support is available Monday to Saturday from 9 AM to 7 PM.
Customers can contact support for order, delivery, payment, return and refund related questions.
